## What this means for the user's sample

The key distinction is now:

1. `Top-side TDTR/FDTR`: top Al/Au transducer on AlN/Si, no buried metal. This may measure effective film/interface response, but AlN kappa and AlN/Si G can be highly correlated unless the lab can show sensitivity, thickness series, or fixed nuisance parameters.
2. `RF NanoTR/PicoTR-style`: rear heating / front detection. This is closer to laser-flash logic and may need optical access through the substrate and a stack designed for RF analysis. If the target is AlN/Si TBC/TBR, the lower/buried side may need a transducer or sandwich design.
3. `3omega`: needs patterned heater/thermometer and electrical isolation. For semiconductive Si and nm films, electrical coupling and line geometry can become the limiting issue.
4. `Thermoreflectance microscopy`: useful for device temperature mapping, not automatically TBC/TBR.
5. `LFA`: useful for bulk/thick films/sheets, not the primary route for nm AlN/Si interface resistance.

## First technical question to send

Before asking about price, send this:

> We need to evaluate thermal boundary conductance/resistance of an AlN/Si(100) interface and/or the cross-plane thermal conductivity of sputtered AlN thin films. The current sample concept is AlN on single-crystal Si(100), with no buried metal layer unless required. Can your TDTR/FDTR/NanoTR/PicoTR/3omega setup identify AlN film kappa and AlN/Si interface conductance separately? If a bottom or rear transducer / sandwich structure is required, please specify the required stack, metal, thickness, substrate transparency condition, chip size, surface roughness, and whether you can provide sensitivity plots and uncertainty bounds before fabrication.
