## Why the list shrank from more than 20 to 17

The earlier larger list mixed three different things:

1. real candidate providers;
2. paper/history-only expertise trails;
3. supporting or false-positive equipment routes.

The 17-item table keeps candidates that still have at least one reason to track: visible equipment/service/product route, direct TDTR/FDTR/3omega publication history, or user-reported decision value. The following were removed from the active provider table, not erased from history:

| Removed or downgraded group | Reason |
|---|---|
| Generic university shared-equipment centers with only TEM/XPS/XRD/FIB/AFM/SIMS | Useful for structural support, but not providers of AlN/Si TBC/TBR. |
| SeoulTech / Pusan / Yonsei / Korea Univ. broad mentions | Some have research or historical clues, but current actual TDTR/FDTR/3omega equipment or service path was not strong enough for first-wave. Korea Univ. evidence is kept indirectly in prior-art notes, not the active 17 except where needed. |
| DGIST / KBSI / thermal-reflectance imaging news | Important for device thermal imaging, but not proven AlN/Si TBC/TBR measurement service. |
| KRISS / KAERI / general NETZSCH LFA holdings | Valuable thermophysical-property institutes, but visible sources were LFA/DSC/DIL/GHP-type, not nm AlN/Si interface TBC/TBR. |
| ZEUS/i-Tube generic LFA entries | Good for bulk or thicker materials; most are not nanometer-film interface conductance tools. |

## Key outreach wording

The essential question is:

> Can your setup identify AlN film cross-plane thermal conductivity and AlN/Si thermal boundary conductance separately for AlN on single-crystal Si(100)? If a bottom/rear transducer or sandwich stack is required, please specify the exact stack, metal, thickness, substrate transparency condition, surface roughness requirement, chip size, and whether you can provide sensitivity plots and uncertainty bounds before fabrication.
