# 11 False-positive and method audit

Audit date: 2026-10-02. Scope: `RESULTS_KO.md` and available reports `01`–`10`; desk audit only, relying on their cited/public-source descriptions. No new web search and no provider contact.

## Findings

- **No material false provider claim found in the reviewed text.** The shortlist consistently separates method or research evidence from external sample acceptance and from successful isolation of AlN/Si interface TBC. NETZSCH's contract TDTR and Korean lab are explicitly not treated as proof that TDTR is run in Korea; KETI's Q&A is described as an inquiry/review, not an accepted measurement; IBS reservation and KIMM equipment listing are not called confirmed commercial services.
- **Method substitutions are generally handled correctly.** KRISS TF-LFA is identified as thin-film diffusivity/conductivity equipment and only a screening lead for TBC; LINSEIS THB, TPS, LFA, imaging and historical 3ω/TDTR papers are not promoted as demonstrated AlN/Si buried-interface services. The reports also flag that KAIST TRAD's cited 3ω work is mainly in-plane and does not establish buried-interface G extraction.
- **One traceability defect:** `RESULTS_KO.md` points to `07_3omega.md` for the 3ω interface/contact-resistance literature review, but no `07_3omega.md` (nor `07_3omega_physics.md`) is present in the inspected folder. The 3ω methodological basis therefore cannot be audited from this packet; avoid implying that a completed review is available until restored or the reference is corrected.
- **Plan/output mismatch:** `00_SCOPE_AND_AGENT_PLAN.md` lists planned `05_portals.md`, `11_exclusions.md`, `12_crosscheck.md`, and `13_final_review.md`, which are absent. This is not itself a provider error, but means the packet does not contain the planned independent cross-check/final review; don't present those work products as completed.
