## Research Workflow

**Literature → Structured Data → Simulation → Experiment → Validation**

Every technical dataset is designed around traceability.

Whenever possible, values are separated by provenance:

**Literature | Experiment | DFT | MD | Monte Carlo | FEM | ML**

Conditions, units, temperature, uncertainty, and reference information are retained so that numerical values can be compared without losing context.

# 13. About Homepage

## About Semiconductor Research Intelligence

Semiconductor Research Intelligence is an independent research platform focused on translating scientific literature, simulation, and experimental data into structured engineering knowledge.

The platform focuses on areas where semiconductor research increasingly overlaps:

materials science,

thin-film processing,

advanced packaging,

thermal transport,

multiscale simulation,

and artificial intelligence.

The objective is simple:

**make semiconductor research more quantitative, traceable, and reusable.**

---

## Research Principles

### Traceability

Every important numerical value should be connected to its origin.

### Reproducibility

Calculations should expose assumptions, inputs, equations, and units.

### Separation of Evidence

Literature, experimental, DFT, MD, Monte Carlo, FEM, and machine-learning outputs should never be treated as equivalent evidence without qualification.

### Uncertainty

Uncertainty and limitations should be visible rather than hidden.

### Engineering Relevance

Scientific analysis should ultimately help answer a physical or engineering question.
