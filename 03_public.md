# Korean public-institute thermal metrology: bounded check (2026-10-02)

Scope: eight web-search queries, limited to official institute pages and public equipment portals. This is an evidence screen, not an appointment or specimen acceptance. No provider was contacted. The requested target is **AlN/Si(100) film and interface thermal resistance**, ideally with TDTR and/or 3ω. An Al optical transducer or 3ω heater on the accessible top surface is provisionally acceptable; **a bottom transducer is not presumed**. The user's c-Si(100)-preserving Al–Al bonded specimen remains an unaccepted, stack-specific proposal. Bulk laser-flash (LFA), transient plane source (TPS), and generic conductivity services do not establish AlN/Si interface conductance capability.

No institution-specific TDTR/3ω or thin-film-interface-TBC offering was verified within the eight-query screen for **KIST, KIMS, ETRI, KRICT, KERI, NNFC, or KANC**. This is not evidence they lack the capability. The iTube hits returned generic conductivity/LFA equipment; a [DEPS thin-film TF-LFA entry](https://deps.dips.or.kr/eps/equipMgr/view/6272) likewise describes laser-flash diffusivity, so neither was promoted to a TBC provider. The [KRISS thin-film in-plane conductivity guide](https://eshop.kriss.re.kr/guide/measurementGuide.do) is relevant metrology context but does not establish cross-plane AlN/Si G service.

**Suggested screening questions, not sent:** (1) Which method is operational—TDTR, FDTR, 3ω—and is external academic work accepted? (2) Can the actual film/wafer order be supplied without a bottom transducer, and who deposits/patterns top Al? (3) What thickness/roughness, substrate, bonding, optical, and heater isolation constraints apply? (4) Can sensitivity analysis identify AlN film κ separately from AlN/Si G and added bond/interface terms, or only effective stack resistance? (5) What are specimen count/size, preparation, cost, lead time, and data/model deliverables? Treat the method and acceptance answer as independent gates.
