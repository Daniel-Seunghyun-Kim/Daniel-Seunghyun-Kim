## Claims that still require confirmation before emailing as facts

- Whether the institution accepts outside user samples or only internal collaborations.
- Whether the lab can measure AlN/Si thermal boundary conductance rather than only effective film thermal resistance.
- Whether top Al transducer deposition is available and whether they require a specific transducer metal/thickness.
- Whether the user's intended AlN thickness is in an identifiable sensitivity window.
- Whether 3omega heater fabrication can be supported on the user's actual wafer/chip format.
- Whether raw data, model files, sensitivity plots, and uncertainty budget can be provided.
- Whether there are contamination restrictions for sputtered AlN/Si wafers.
- Whether costs are service fees, collaboration-only, or unavailable.
